Methods

IoU of the boxes with a mass threshold

IoU between the union of the predicted boxes and the union of the GT boxes per annotated plane, mean per event, then per class and over the 13 classes. Mean = mean of the two datasets. Each configuration is on its own detector.

Binary head of the detectors

The laboratory's OVAD protocol, 30 s guard, Youden threshold on the FS-UCF-Crime validation transferred to MIVIA-Crime.

Classifiers of the routing

Choice of the class on the test chunks selected for the spatial evaluation (13 classes, Normal left out before the argmax). They do not describe the quality of the boxes.

Units and means

Every metric is computed per event, on its images; then it is averaged over the events of each class and over the 13 classes. An image is a distinct pair (annotated plane, temporal plane of the predicted chunk) with its GT boxes. Per event only the images with a GT are used. «Mean of the two datasets» is the plain mean of the two values.

The choices among configurations, variants and thresholds are made on the test set: they are exploratory.

Regimes: annotated chunks and real use

annotated chunks
boxes are produced on every test chunk with a spatial GT, whatever the detector says (27 October). The «· dataset» metrics use the images with a GT only.
real use
every chunk of the test videos is seen, and boxes are produced only on the chunks the binary head of the configuration's detector flags: score ≥ τ, with τ the Youden point on the validation of that detector (transferred to MIVIA-Crime). An annotated chunk that is not flagged has no box. Videos: all 290 of FS-UCF-Crime; on MIVIA-Crime the 39 with a spatial GT and 39 normal ones, chosen as the annotated ones, by increasing number of base planes.
negative images
in real use, the chunk planes of the frames outside every temporal event: no object, every detection is a false positive. They enter the «· dataset» metrics (false positives and frames of RBDC, false positives of the AP); the frames inside an event without a reviewed spatial GT do not. Per event nothing changes, except for the unflagged chunks.
false alarms
in the IoU view, the share of negative images that get at least one box: the IoU looks only at the frames with a GT.

IoU thresholds

@t
the metric at a single IoU threshold t, from 0.05 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05.
[a:s:b]
mean over the thresholds from a to b with step s, both ends included. Step 0.1 is the main one (the THUMOS14 convention for [0.1:0.5], [0.3:0.7] and [0.1:0.7]); step 0.05 is a check.
{a,b,…}
mean over those values only. [0.5:0.1:0.7] = {0.5,0.6,0.7}, unlike {0.5,0.7}.
[0.5:0.05:0.95]
the COCO AP.

Every mean is computed per event and then aggregated: with every threshold present for every event it equals the mean of the values at each threshold.

Metrics

AP@t
COCO AP at threshold t: one-to-one matching by decreasing score, 101 recall points, precision envelope. AP@[a:s:b] is the mean over thresholds.
RBDC@t
Region-Based Detection Criterion (Ramachandra & Jones, WACV 2020), with the script of Georgescu et al. (2021). A GT region is detected if a detection covers it with IoU ≥ t; a detection may detect more than one; a detection covering only regions already detected is neither true nor false; one covering none is a false positive. RBDC is the area under the RBDR–FPR curve for FPR (false positives per frame) between 0 and 1. RBDC = RBDC@0.1, the threshold of the literature. It is the main metric.
RBDR@t
Region-Based Detection Rate with every detection, with no threshold on the score: the fraction of GT regions covered with IoU ≥ t by at least one detection. It is a recall on the regions of the events and does not penalize false alarms. RBDR = RBDR@0.1.
mRBDC, mRBDR
means over thresholds, as AP@[a:s:b]: mRBDC@[0.1:0.1:0.5], mRBDR@{0.3,0.5,0.7}, …
· dataset
AP, RBDC and RBDR computed as in the RBDC paper: a single curve over every image of the dataset, with the scores compared across chunks. No class means. In real use they include the negative images.

RBDR is exact on the annotated frames alone too, because it counts GT regions only. RBDC is not: in the literature the false positives are counted on every frame, normal ones included. With the annotated chunks those of the normal frames are missing, so RBDC is optimistic; the «· dataset» RBDC of real use counts them.

GT readings

union
one object per image, the union of the areas of every GT box (as the IoU metric).
touching
the GT boxes whose pixels are 8-connected (overlapping, sharing a side or just a corner) are joined, transitively; the object is the union of the areas. It is the rule of the predicted components. Our own definition.
instances
every GT box is an object.

The three readings change the objects to find: the numbers of different readings are not comparable with each other.

Box variants and scores

union
cumulative union: at every confidence threshold, a single region per image, the union of the concepts above the threshold. The curve is not monotone: the AP takes the precision envelope, RBDC the highest RBDR reachable at each FPR; RBDR uses every concept.
component
every 8-connected component of every concept, with the mass of the component.
concept-union / concept-box
per concept, the union of the areas of its components / the box enclosing them.
component-concept-score
the components as separate instances, with the mass of the concept.
largest-component
the largest component of each concept.
nms / softnms / wbf
NMS at IoU 0.3, 0.5, 0.7; Soft-NMS (σ 0.5, cut 0.001); WBF (IoU 0.55, mean), on component or concept-box.
mass / share
score = sum of the positive IG of the concept (or of the component) / the same mass divided by the total of the chunk.
