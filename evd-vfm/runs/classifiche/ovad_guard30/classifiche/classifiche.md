# OVAD rankings

Main collection: one version per model. Among the versions of the same training extended in continuation (one, three and four epochs; three and four for the combinations) the higher mean of the UCF/MIVIA means at seed 42 is chosen (tie: fewer epochs). It is a selection on the test set, not on validation. The version stays fixed for both datasets and all seeds; the name is plain. The choices are in `selezione_versioni.json` and the alternatives in [../classifiche_tutte_versioni/classifiche.md](../classifiche_tutte_versioni/classifiche.md).

All 15 metrics and the protocol mean, in percent; lower FPR is better, all other metrics higher is better. Ordered by mean. No average across seeds in the following tables.

Mean = (AUC + macro AUC_a + row F1 + (1 − row FPR) + video F1 + event recall) / 6. Mean of means = (UCF mean + MIVIA mean) / 2, equal weight per dataset.

c=8 s, g=30 s, h_test=4 s, h_val=8 s (warning expected from the code), T=32 s. Youden on the UCF validation rows, transferred to MIVIA. Rows are not chunks; a video can contribute both to the event part and to the clean part. Thresholds in the original results.json, counts and provenance in the per-seed CSV/JSON.
