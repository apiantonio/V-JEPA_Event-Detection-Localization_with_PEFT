## 6. Cross-dataset: MIVIA-Crime

The selected weights of each cell, run over the lab's private test set (270 videos, 3485 chunks) without any training or re-tuning. Seed(s) 42 only, so **these are single-seed numbers** next to multi-seed means, and the delta is paired on the same seed rather than taken against the UCF mean.

Read the AP through its **lift** (AP / positive-chunk rate), not raw: MIVIA-Crime is 30.65% positive chunks against 16.36% on UCF-Crime, so a higher AP here is floor, not skill. `Chunk F1` uses a threshold **transferred** from UCF-Crime and never re-estimated, so it measures ranking *plus* how well the calibration survives; `F1 oracle` is Youden computed on MIVIA-Crime itself and is **not protocol** — it exists only to separate the two.

`Mean AUC` is the plain average of the two chunk AUCs, the UCF-Crime one taken **at the same seed**. It is the single number to rank by when the model has to hold up at home *and* on a domain it has never seen — ⚠️ but weighting the two datasets equally is a **choice**, not a fact: both terms are right next to it for anyone who wants to weight them differently.
