1. Choose the video and the starting point

The videos are on your computer. The collection on the left can be filtered by name, class, dataset (“UCF only” or “MIVIA only”) and state. “All videos” also includes the normal ones; the initial “With events” filter shows only the anomalous ones. “Sort” orders the list by name (the project order), by annotated class (from the events, not from the file name), by number of events or by number of VTCD planes, ascending or descending; ties fall back to the name order. Each card shows events and planes; the choice is kept in this browser. “Statistics”, at the top, summarises progress: completed videos, events, planes and frames out of the total, by class and by video, with the distributions and the planes completed day by day. A unit is completed when all its frames are reviewed or excluded. Clicking a row of the per-video table opens that video; “Export CSV per video” saves the table with the current filters. Enter the reviewer name before saving. If a video is missing, the tool flags it: the file must be relinked.

The proposed boxes are immediately selectable and editable; opening a plane does not mean reviewing it. You can choose another source in “Starting point” or work from scratch. Existing corrections, deletions included, take precedence over the suggestions.

2. Edit the boxes, the grid and the properties

A click selects; drag to move, use the eight handles to resize. B enables drawing, V selection. A left double click on the video creates a square box centred on the point and enables Draw, even from Select. A click on a box, even from Draw, selects it and allows moving and resizing right away. The resize handles of the selected box stay above all the other boxes, even when they overlap. Shift+click adds or removes a box from the selection. Ctrl+C copies the selected boxes; Ctrl+V adds them to the current plane or frame of the same video, at the same coordinates, with new free IDs. Pasted boxes are selected and in draft, ready to move; Ctrl+Z/Y undoes and redoes the paste. You can paste several times and navigate between planes with compatible events. The box clipboard lasts until the tab is closed or reloaded; in text fields Ctrl+C/V keeps the normal copy/paste. Del deletes. In the details you can type exact coordinates and choose the event class when it is ambiguous. The object type is not requested.

Right click on a box, on the video or in the list, opens the menu: “Fit to tokens”, numeric ID, colour, duplication and deletion. Reassign IDs, above the box list, renumbers all the boxes of the current plane or frame from 1, without gaps, in list order. It keeps the selection, the geometry and the internal identity of the boxes; the change goes back to draft, can be undone with Ctrl+Z/Y and saved with Ctrl+S. If the IDs are already consecutive nothing changes. The ID must be free in the current plane; it is distinct from the internal identity kept for provenance. “Apply ID and colour” confirms the fields; Ctrl+S applies and saves them even with the menu open. ID and colour are exported and can be undone. Saving also stores the token space of each box, derived from the pixels without replacing them. The optional track links the same object at different times for interpolation.

Grid (G) shows or hides the tokens. A token is 16×16 pixels on the model input: at 384×384 that is 24×24 cells, projected onto the whole original video. For example, at 320×240 a cell is about 13.3×10 native pixels, not 16×16. The tool uses the source geometry; without a source it uses that of the EDP-v2 protocol.

Fit to tokens moves the four edges to the nearest lines, keeping at least one cell and staying inside the video. The right and bottom coordinates are inclusive. The original stays visible in amber while you edit the geometry; H hides or shows it. +/− zoom the video in and out, 0 fits it again. Zoom does not change the coordinates. On the video, the trackpad pinch controls zoom. Two-finger scrolling pans vertically, horizontally or diagonally when the zoomed video exceeds the available space; the mouse wheel scrolls without zooming.

Token (T) overlays the discretised version in light blue: it includes every token sharing at least one pixel with the drawn box. The edges extend outwards to the grid, so the token box fully contains the pixel box. A box already aligned to the grid stays identical. Token coordinates are shown in the box details and have exclusive end bounds. The pixel box stays editable and the projection updates immediately. Ctrl+S also refreshes the derived fields of annotations saved with the previous rule, without touching them. “Fit to tokens” instead changes the pixel box: use it only to align the original too.

Thin (L) shows one-pixel outlines, without fill or shadows, even on selected boxes. Interiors stay clickable; the mode only changes the display.

3. Work by temporal plane

In “VTCD planes”, a correction applies to all the frames of the same stretch. The arrows move to the previous or next plane; Shift+arrows jump ten units. Boxes stay constant until the plane changes. Planes without boxes must be checked too; an empty plane does not mean a completed review.

The timeline reaches any frame; [ and ] move between events. Space starts or stops playback. The selector offers 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× and 4× in both Frames and VTCD planes. The duration follows the native FPS; a plane stays visible for the duration of the stretch it represents. In Planes, the gaps between events are skipped. M toggles the two modes. Even with “No proposal / work from scratch” each event has its base VTCD planes: windows of the EDP-v2 protocol, for each frame the window with the most annotated frames (as in the VTCD sources) and 16 planes per window, without proposed boxes. “Details” expands metadata, legend and events; “Minimize” gives the space back to the video. “Next to review” looks for the work still open.

“Frames” allows single-frame exceptions. If a plane contains different or partial corrections, collective edits are blocked: you can continue frame by frame or explicitly choose “Unify plane…”. Unifying copies the current boxes and note over the whole stretch, keeps the history and can be undone.

“Copy from the previous plane” (P) needs a contiguous, uniform plane: it uses the existing corrections, or the original proposals if the plane was never edited. If the current plane contains boxes, it asks whether to replace them. Cancel in the dialog keeps the boxes; Ctrl+Z reverts a replacement already made. “Apply to several planes…” (Shift+P) carries the current plane boxes, all or only the selected ones, onto a range of planes chosen by number: it replaces or adds, leaves everything as draft, skips planes with incompatible classes or different per-frame corrections and protects reviewed and excluded planes unless they are explicitly included. “From stretch start / To stretch end” stop at the first gap between planes. Ctrl+Z undoes the whole operation. In Frames mode, “Propagate / interpolate” generates drafts within a single event: copying uses the selection; interpolation needs unique tracks matching at both ends. Reviewed and excluded frames are protected. There is no automatic tracking.

On opening, the event frames start preloading progressively; on normal videos the whole video is preloaded. The counter shows the progress. The disk cache is limited to 1 GiB and the browser cache to 48 MiB: large videos may have a partial cache. The work stays usable while loading. In Frames, playback follows the elapsed time and may skip intermediate images at high speeds; the arrows still advance by exactly one frame. In Planes, if decoding cannot keep up, playback slows down without skipping planes. Changing video stops the previous preload. The cache can be regenerated and contains no annotations.

4. Save or confirm the review

CommandEffect
Save · Ctrl+SProposes by default “Reassign all IDs and save”, already selected and triggered with Enter: it renumbers from 1 the boxes of every annotated plane/frame of the open video, in list order, and saves everything. It keeps geometry, single-frame corrections and review state. You can also reassign and confirm the drafts, or save/confirm without renumbering. Renumbering and the optional confirmation are undone together with Ctrl+Z/Y. It stays on the current plane; “Cancel” closes without renumbering or saving. Proposals never edited stay out.
Confirm and advance · Ctrl+EnterDeclares the whole plane (or frame) checked, saves and moves to the next one to review. Reviewed frames enter the exported ground truth.

Collective confirmation also includes empty drafts, leaves out the excluded frames and never accepts proposals that were never edited. It can be undone as a single action; boxes with an ambiguous class must be resolved first. The badge distinguishes “To review”, “Draft”, “Reviewed” and “Excluded”. Editing a reviewed annotation moves it back to draft; renumbering the whole video on save keeps its state. “Clear boxes” records an explicit empty frame, to be confirmed if correct. “Exclude plane/frame” requires a note and excludes the stretch from the ground truth. Notes help to remember occlusions or doubts.

Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes: they also work in the editor fields and after saving and changing video, as long as the page stays open. A new edit after an undo discards the Redo branch. ID, colour and fitting to tokens can be undone too. Navigation does not change the annotations.

5. Import for one video or for the whole collection

“Import +” in the starting point adds a boxes.json / boxes_per_frame.csv pair to the open video. The required variant is “annotati”; both the historical boxes and the per-class VTCD boxes are supported. The original sources are kept separate from the human work.

Import collection… in the left bar works on all videos. You can use the candidates already registered or choose a folder with the JSON/CSV pairs, also in subfolders. Only these files are read: the videos are not uploaded. Each pair must sit in the same folder; the content identifies the video, not the folder name. If names are ambiguous, choose the dataset.

Filter the candidates and select the desired pairs. The filter changes the view, not the selection. Press “Check selection”, review the summary and then “Import checked”. Pairs already present are not duplicated; errors are shown per file. “Stop” ends after the current pair: the imported ones stay available and you can repeat the batch.

The “Use as starting point” option applies only to videos without corrections and with a single source chosen in the batch. If you import several sources for the same video, choose the one to use in the video menu. No correction or confirmation is overwritten. Importing adds an immutable source; Ctrl+Z undoes box edits, it does not remove imported files.

External sources: Liu & Ma 2019

In “Starting point” choose the “Liu & Ma 2019” source. “First sample” goes to the first box; in “Source samples” mode, the arrows step only through the frames annotated by the source. M toggles samples and frames. The samples are sparse, often 16 frames apart, and are not VTCD planes: no box is extended automatically to the gaps.

Boxes are editable right away where you have no annotations. Your corrections always take precedence; “Use source boxes” offers to replace only the current frame, as draft and with Ctrl+Z/Y. To move a box to a VTCD plane you can copy it, change source, reach the plane and paste it with Ctrl+C/V. The provenance stays attached.

The ready collection contains 2,833 boxes on 139 videos of the UCF test set. The 269 boxes outside the lab events are in the audit.json report; Stealing019 is not imported. On Arrest030 the 104 samples cover only part of the Arrest sequence. They are regions of the action, sometimes with several subjects together: they must be adapted to the criterion of separate people and objects. The authors do not document the exact conventions: the file number is kept as index and the edges are clamped to the video. Do not treat them as confirmed ground truth without review.

To reimport: “Import collection” → “Choose folder”, select runs/annotazioni_spaziali/fonti_esterne/liu_ma_2019, check and import. The original file and its provenance are kept. Importing does not confirm boxes and does not replace corrections.

6. All shortcuts

The table is generated from the same definition used by the keyboard. In text fields the plain keys are for typing: save, confirm and the editor undo/redo stay available. Ctrl+E is not a tool command. For the menu you can also use right click or Shift+F10 on the selected box.

7. Keep, recover and export the work

The work is saved with Ctrl+S after the choice in the dialog; confirming, changing video and exporting also save before proceeding. The indicator at the top flags unsaved changes or errors. In case of a conflict between tabs, the other work is not overwritten: download the proposed recovery copy before reloading.

“Export project” downloads a ZIP with a reopenable project, database and history, sources, drafts and exclusions. JSON, CSV and COCO ground truth include only the reviewed frames, those without boxes included. The videos are not duplicated in the ZIP. Pixel and token coordinates, visible ID, colour and provenance accompany the boxes; the original temporal annotations stay unchanged.

To resume a backup, extract project.json and annotations.sqlite3 into a new folder and start the tool with --project FOLDER. The browser copy is applied with --recover FILE; a previous revision is recovered with --restore-revision VIDEO_ID REVISION. These operations create a new revision. To relink a moved video use --relink VIDEO_ID FILE, with the server stopped.
