LOCALIZATION MOVIES

Localization movies

Each movie is one localization method on one annotated video, in the demonstrator layout: the online anomaly score and alarms, the chunk the boxes come from with its TP/FP/FN/TN verdict, the annotated events with the evaluated IoU of the method, and the whole-video timeline.

Choose a video

IoU: the evaluated IoU of each method on an annotated event (as in the IoU ranking), one coloured dot per method; a video's IoU is the mean over its events. "Best" and "mean" use the methods shown, and the gap is the first shown method minus the second. Events without an IoU go last. Chunk lane: TP FP FN TN, against the run threshold. Boxes: solid and numbered = the method; dashed ochre = spatial ground truth the IoU was computed on. Boxes exist only inside annotated intervals. "Anomaly score (online)" and the alarm follow the demonstrator (a window speaks when it closes); "chunk of the boxes" is the chunk containing the frame, as the evaluator projects boxes, so the two can differ. Methods were chosen on the test set (exploratory).
