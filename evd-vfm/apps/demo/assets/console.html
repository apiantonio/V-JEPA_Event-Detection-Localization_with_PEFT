How to use

1. Choose the model from the menu.

2. Select a library video and press Open. List events shows the annotated events one by one and opens the video at the chosen event; Sort by orders either list by a metric of that video or event, with the same numbers the player shows.

3. Press Play. Click the timeline or an alarm to reach it. Chunk outcome colours the chunks of the timeline as TP, FP, FN or TN.

The score measures the anomaly. The threshold comes from the model validation. Classes, when present, are dataset annotations.

«New analysis» runs the model on a file from the PC and needs the trained weights. On CPU it can take a long time.

To stop the service, press Ctrl+C in the launcher window.

Streaming replays the saved scores progressively: running windows fill up progressively and the score appears only when they close. «Live analysis» instead computes new scores on the PC.

Streaming supports all shortcuts, timeline seeking and speed changes too. The Annotations button shows or hides the dataset intervals on the timeline and on the video. In live analysis use the Start video / Pause button. Player shortcuts are disabled while a field, a menu or a button is in use.
